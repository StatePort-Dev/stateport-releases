# Security policy

Please report suspected vulnerabilities in StatePort, its installers, update delivery, or artifacts **privately**. Do not open a public issue or pull request, and do not post exploit details, credentials, personal data, or sensitive logs in public.

Use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** option on this repository's [Security Advisories page](https://github.com/StatePort-Dev/stateport-releases/security/advisories). Include the affected version and platform, a concise reproduction, the potential impact, and a safe way to contact you. Share proof of concept material only in the private report. The maintainers will review the report and coordinate disclosure and fixes there.

For non-security installer, update, or download problems, use the [public issue form](https://github.com/StatePort-Dev/stateport-releases/issues/new/choose), after removing private information from logs.
